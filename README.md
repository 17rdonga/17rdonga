# Rohan Donga
Economics undergraduate at the University of Cambridge interested in Statistics and Microeconomics.
## Projects:
- ADR premium tracker
- IMC Prosperity algorithmic trading competition: ranked 7th in the UK and 100th in the world out of 12,000 entries. Programmed mean reversion, market making and momentum trading strategies using Avellaneda-Stoikov market  making. I frequently used backtesting and visualisers to understand price patterns and prevent overfitting.
- Consumer Confidence as a leading indicator: created a consumer confidence index using Google Trends data. Employed Granger Causality and unit root tests to understand whether Consumer confidence statistically 'leads' other economic indicators such as GDP and inflation.
- AI bubble prediction (in progress):
    - Component 1: Using econometric tests such as GSADF to test points where AI stock prices have severely jumped in comparison to their DCF-implied fair values. Then I am experimenting with ML techniques to develop a methodology to predict if and when bubbles will burst using historic bubble data
    - Component 2: Using options data and inverse Black Scholes to derive implied volatilities. Then I am using Breeden Litzenberger method and stochastic smoothing to derive risk neutral probability densities for AI equities, funds and indicies. By examining the evolution of left tail probabilities over time I hope to understand how investor prediction of an AI crash has evolved over time.
- Double/Debiased ML to analyse the effect of leverage on equity volatility: ML is useful in statistical inference to predict a function for the control variables, such as firm size etc. To eradicate bias in the coefficient of interest a Double ML approach is taken to achieve orthogonality and an asymptotically normal coefficient - (Chetverikov et al, 2018)
